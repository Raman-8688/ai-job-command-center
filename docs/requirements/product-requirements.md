# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Vision
**AI Job Command Center** is a private, intelligent operational platform for professional software engineers to organize, evaluate, accelerate, and optimize their job hunt. It replaces chaotic spreadsheets, scattered browser tabs, and disconnected email threads with a unified command center.

## 2. Target Persona
- **Role:** Mid-to-Senior / Lead Software Engineer.
- **Goals:** High response rate, targeted applications matching verified capabilities, zero missed interview/OA deadlines, proactive recruiter follow-ups.
- **Pain Points:** Repetitive resume tailoring, losing track of email threads across dozens of active applications, generic ATS rejections, anxiety over missing online assessments.

## 3. Core Product Pillars
1. **Factual Grounding (Truthfulness):** Zero tolerance for fabricated experience.
2. **Human Control:** The system advises, analyzes, and drafts; the human user approves and executes external actions.
3. **Operational Visibility:** Complete traceability of every application from initial discovery to offer negotiation.
4. **Efficiency:** Drastic reduction in manual preparation time per high-value application.

## 4. Key Success Metrics (KPIs)
- **Time to Apply (High Quality):** Reduced from ~45 minutes to <10 minutes per tailored application.
- **Interview Conversion Rate:** Target > 20% conversion from application to technical round.
- **Missed Deadlines:** Zero missed OA or interview confirmation windows.
- **Data Integrity:** 0% hallucinated skills or invalid employment claims in generated resumes.
