# Data Privacy Policy & Compliance

## 1. Scope of Private Data
The application processes:
- Full personal legal names, private emails, phone numbers, and physical addresses.
- Detailed employment compensation history and salary expectations.
- Confidential recruiter conversations and pending offer terms.

## 2. Privacy Directives
- **Zero Third-Party Data Sharing:** The application never sells, aggregates, or exports user job search data to third-party ad networks or analytics services.
- **Local Isolation:** Telemetry is disabled; all metrics remain on local disk/database.
- **Data Export & Deletion:** The user has the complete right and programmatic ability to export their full database snapshot or execute a hard purge of all data.
